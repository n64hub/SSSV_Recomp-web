HD World Textures

Optional high-resolution replacements for level sprites and sign faces.
Toggle HD World Textures in the texture-pack section of Graphics settings.
This option is independent of HD Text and HD Textures (HUD/menu artwork).

Includes plants, flowers, foliage, cacti, rocks, collectibles, butterfly views,
energy animation, world effects, shadows, trails, Earth/Silicon Valley signs,
and the three illustrated sheep/dog tutorial sign faces in Have A Nice Day.
All six native mip levels of those three tutorial materials use their HD faces.
The identical jumping-sheep material in Fat Bear Mountain is also covered.
Original geometry, sprite placement and native animation order are retained.
Blank frames, utility textures and unrelated 3D materials keep native data.

Artwork was made with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, using the
original sprites as visual references. Original game art belongs to its owners.
The runtime replacement manifest contains texture identifiers, not ROM data.
No game ROM or save files are part of this texture folder.

rt64.json registers the replacements. artworks.json describes animation-sheet
regions. PROVENANCE.json records the final artwork prompts and native names.

Other mipmapped sign materials elsewhere have not yet been audited; this pack
does not claim every sign in the game is replaced.
