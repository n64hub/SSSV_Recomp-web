HD Hud

16x resolution green seven-segment HUD digits with faint unlit segments, plus health and energy bars, with concentric circular lights and rings, equal 3.2-native-pixel silver rails/end caps, and consistent upper-left lighting.

Graphics > HD Hud: On (default) or Off. Independent of HD Text. Off restores native textures. Native drawing still controls colours, damage flash, animations and HUD placement.

Rebuild: python tools/build_hd_hud.py
Preview: python tools/preview_hd_hud.py
Requires numpy, Pillow, xxhash and the supported local US 1.0 ROM. The ROM is not included. The fill ramp is derived from that local ROM.
